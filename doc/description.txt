Skip Wait is a Chrome extension for skip wait, countdown bypass, and link shortener bypass. It skips countdown timers and waiting pages on supported URL shorteners and file hosts. If a site still asks you to wait or click Continue, Skip Wait does that for you, then opens the destination or file.

Start with no key: 5 free bypasses each day. The count resets at midnight on your device. A short link that uses a few pages still counts as one use. For no daily limit, get a 30-minute trial or $1.50 per month on EAS Store, paste the key in the popup, and tap Activate. One key per device. A live trial or monthly key does not use the daily free count.

Skip Wait is a FastForward and Universal Bypass alternative. It is not FastForward or Universal Bypass. It is skip wait in Chrome: bypass timers, skip countdown, and link shortener bypass on matching pages only. Other sites stay unchanged. No paste tool and no site-by-site setup.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
What Skip Wait Does
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Skip Wait runs when you open a supported delay page. It either skips the wait or finishes the Continue steps for you.

• Skip wait and skip countdown on delay and download-timer pages
• Countdown bypass and bypass timers on please-wait screens
• Link shortener bypass on URL shorteners and short-link chains
• Direct download when a file host hides the real link behind a generating wait
• Safelink, locker, landing, and multi-step waits on supported hosts

Privacy
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Skip Wait does not inject ads.

On this device it stores short-lived progress on multi-step chains and the cached site list. Cookie reads happen only on certain supported pages where the destination is in an HttpOnly cookie, used locally to finish that jump. License checks go to EAS Store only when you activate, validate, or remove a key. The public hosts.json list on GitHub is fetched when the extension starts and when you tap New domains.
